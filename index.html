Happy Couple - Budget, Save & Split Together

Happy Couple - Budget, Save & Split Together is a mobile app that helps two people manage a shared household budget. Track your incomes and expenses, split costs the way that's fair for you, settle up whenever you like, and save together. Use the app on your phone, or open the same budget in Google Sheets on your computer — with all your data kept privately in your own Google account.

See what it does

Built for two

Everything a couple needs, nothing you don't

Happy Couple keeps your money life shared and simple — without ads, spreadsheets you have to build yourself, or handing your data to a stranger.

🔐

Your data, your Drive

Your budget lives in a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive. We don't run a server that stores it — it's yours to keep, edit on your computer, or delete.

⚖️

Split it your way

Share costs in proportion to your incomes, or set a fixed ratio such as 60/40. With Premium, any category can have its own split — say, 50/50 on groceries.

🤝

Settle up any time

See who owes whom as the month goes on. Settle the full amount or just part of it, add a note, and look back at every payment in your settlement history.

🐖

Save together

Set aside savings each month and watch your history build. The app suggests an amount from your month's numbers, using a percentage you choose.

🗂️

Categories that fit you

Name your own spending categories and give each one an icon, so Rent, Groceries and Date night are easy to spot at a glance.

💞

Pair in seconds

Invite your partner with a link or a QR code. You both read and write the same budget, each from your own phone.

📅

Month by month, year by year

Incomes, expenses, savings and settlements, laid out month by month across the years, so you always know where you stand.

🎨

Make it yours

Light or dark mode, colour themes for the app, and your budget in euros, dollars or pounds.

🚫

No ads, ever

No banners, no advertising trackers, and no selling your data. The free plan is genuinely useful on its own.

Private by design

We can't see your budget

There's no Happy Couple server holding your finances. The app talks straight to your Google account, and only touches the file it created for you.

  • Stored in your Drive — not on our servers.
  • Limited Google access — the app can only see the budget file it made (the drive.file scope), nothing else in your Drive.
  • Shared only with your partner — and only when you choose to invite them.
  • No ads, no selling data — ever.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Happy Couple free?

Yes. Logging incomes and expenses, splitting, settling up, savings and your latest 3 months of history are free with no time limit. Premium is optional.

Do we both need to buy Premium?

No. When one of you has Premium, it unlocks for both of you in your shared budget.

Where is our money data stored?

In a Google Sheet in the Google Drive of the person who created the budget, shared with their partner. Happy Couple has no server that stores your budget, and the app can only access the file it created.

What happens to our data if we stop Premium?

Nothing is deleted. The app goes back to showing your latest 3 months, and everything older stays in your Google Sheet. Upgrade again and it all reappears in the app.

Can we split costs unevenly?

Yes. Split in proportion to your incomes, or set a fixed ratio such as 60/40. With Premium, individual categories can have their own split, for example 50/50 on groceries while rent follows your incomes.

Can we edit our budget on a computer?

Yes. Your budget is a Google Sheet, so you can open it in Google Sheets on any computer to make bulk edits, sort and filter expenses, or review your history. Changes stay in sync with the app.

Do I need to know how to use spreadsheets?

No. The app creates and fills in the sheet for you, and you can do everything from your phone. The spreadsheet is there for when you want it.

Is there an iPhone version?

Happy Couple is launching on Android through Google Play first.

Ready to budget together?

Free to start, private by design, and set up in a couple of minutes.
