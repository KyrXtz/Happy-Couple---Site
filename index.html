Happy Couple - Budget, Save & Split Together

Happy Couple - Budget, Save & Split Together is a mobile app that helps two people manage a shared household budget. Track your incomes and expenses, split shared costs and settle up each month, and save together — with all your data kept privately in your own Google account.

Built for two

Everything a couple needs, nothing you don't

Happy Couple keeps your money life shared and simple — without ads, spreadsheets you have to build yourself, or handing your data to a stranger.

🔐

Your data, your Drive

Your budget lives in a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive. We don't run a server that stores it — it's yours to keep, export, or delete.

🧾

Split & settle up

Log shared expenses, see each person's share, and know exactly who owes whom at the end of every month.

🐖

Save together

Set aside savings each month and watch your history build. The app even suggests an amount based on your spending.

💞

Pair in seconds

Invite your partner with a link or a QR code. Both of you read and write the same budget in real time.

📊

Clear, honest numbers

Incomes, expenses, savings and settlement — laid out month by month so you always know where you stand.

✨

Free to start

Your latest months are free forever. Premium unlocks your full history and unlimited spending categories when you're ready.

Private by design

We can't see your budget

There's no Happy Couple server holding your finances. The app talks straight to your Google account, and only touches the file it created for you.

  • Stored in your Drive — not on our servers.
  • Limited Google access — the app can only see the budget file it made (the drive.file scope), nothing else in your Drive.
  • Shared only with your partner — and only when you choose to invite them.
  • No ads, no selling data — ever.
